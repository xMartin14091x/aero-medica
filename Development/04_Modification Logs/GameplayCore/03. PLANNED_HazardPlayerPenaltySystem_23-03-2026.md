# SYN-MOD-01 — Hazard Player Penalty System

**Phase:** Post-8 (Modification)
**Team:** Syndicate
**Status:** [x] Complete
**Complexity:** Medium
**Depends on:** None
**Blocks:** None

## Scope
Add player penalty mechanics to the hazard zone system:
1. Player accumulates hazard exposure time; after 15s total, emit `player_incapacitated` signal ending the scenario
2. While player stands in a hazard zone, patients in that same zone deteriorate 25% faster (1.25x damage multiplier)
3. HazardSystem tracks total player hazard exposure time for telemetry
4. ScenarioManager wires hazard time into results dict
5. AI reviewer mentions reckless hazard behavior in performance review

- [x] hazard_zone.gd: `player_incapacitated` signal emits after 15s threshold
- [x] hazard_zone.gd: 1.25x damage multiplier on patients when player is co-located
