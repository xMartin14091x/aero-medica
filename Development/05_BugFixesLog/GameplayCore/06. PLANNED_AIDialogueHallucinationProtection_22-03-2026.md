# BUG-GC-06 — AI Dialogue Lacks Hallucination Protection

**Date:** 22-03-2026
**Reported by:** Chief Manager Martin (pre-implementation review)
**Severity:** High
**Status:** `[ ] PENDING`

---

## Symptom

The Ollama dialogue client builds a system prompt from patient persona data (SAMPLE history, medical state) but does not include explicit grounding constraints. The LLM can invent symptoms, medications, allergies, or history not present in the patient data. With temperature 0.7, creative confabulation is likely.

Example risk: Player asks "Do you have chest pain?" — AI responds "Yes, terrible chest pain" even though the patient's data has no chest pain listed. This teaches incorrect clinical reasoning.

## Root Cause

`ollama_dialogue_client.gd:_build_system_prompt()` (line 128-161):
- Includes patient identity, medical state, SAMPLE history
- Includes behavior rules (stay in character, short responses, etc.)
- Rule: "Answer questions about your history truthfully based on the SAMPLE data above"
- **MISSING:** No explicit constraint against inventing unlisted symptoms/history
- **MISSING:** No instruction for handling questions about unlisted conditions
- Temperature 0.7 allows creative drift

## Fix

1. Add grounding rules to system prompt:
   - "ONLY report symptoms, conditions, and history listed in your data above. Do NOT invent new symptoms."
   - "If asked about something not in your data, say you don't know, deflect, or say it doesn't apply."
   - "Never add new allergies, medications, or medical history beyond what is listed."
2. Lower temperature from 0.7 to 0.4 for more deterministic responses
3. Optionally: add per-patient negative constraint list (conditions they explicitly do NOT have)

## Files

- `scripts/ai/ollama/ollama_dialogue_client.gd` — `_build_system_prompt()`, temperature setting

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] System prompt includes explicit "do not invent" grounding rules
- [ ] Temperature reduced to 0.4
- [ ] When asked about unlisted symptoms, AI deflects or says "I don't think so" / "No"
- [ ] When asked about listed symptoms, AI responds accurately from SAMPLE data
