## Overall Assessment
Solid performance on the Cardiac Arrest scenario. Cardiac arrest was recognised and CPR was initiated. AED was applied and shock delivered. RED triage assigned correctly. Protocol adherence: approximately 75%. Some delays in the chain of survival but no critical protocol violations. 0 critical errors detected.

## Strengths
- Cardiac arrest correctly identified — pulseless, apnoeic state confirmed
- CPR initiated and 30:2 ratio followed
- AED applied and VFib rhythm shocked appropriately
- RED triage correctly assigned
- Airway managed with head-tilt chin-lift

## Areas for Improvement
- Time from confirming cardiac arrest to first compression was over 20 seconds — aim for under 10 seconds. Every second without compressions reduces survival probability
- AED application was delayed — in VFib, early defibrillation is the single most important intervention
- There was a prolonged pause between AED shock and CPR resumption — resume compressions immediately after shock delivery
- Call for help should be initiated earlier — ideally before or simultaneously with starting CPR

## Critical Errors
No critical errors detected.

## Recommendations
- Practice the sequence until it is automatic: confirm arrest, call for help, start CPR, apply AED — all within 30 seconds
- Focus on minimising hands-off time — any pause in compressions drops perfusion pressure
- Review the AED prompts carefully — the device guides you through rhythm analysis and shock delivery
- Time yourself: from scenario start to first shock should be under 90 seconds for optimal outcomes
