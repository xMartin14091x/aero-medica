## Recommendations
- Any patient with a compromised or obstructed airway is automatically RED — regardless of consciousness level or other findings
- In fire scenarios, assume inhalation injury when soot is visible around the nose or mouth
- Practice the airway intervention sequence: identify compromise, perform head-tilt chin-lift, reassess, apply oxygen
- Review the triage criteria for RED: abnormal breathing, compromised airway, absent pulse, or altered consciousness
